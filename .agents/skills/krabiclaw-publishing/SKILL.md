# Publishing KrabiClaw news

Read [product-profile.md](../../../docs/marketing/product-profile.md) for verified
facts and Paul's voice and [PRODUCT.md](../../../PRODUCT.md) for current policy.
Use [write-help-docs](../write-help-docs/SKILL.md) for customer instructions and
[krabiclaw-marketing](../krabiclaw-marketing/SKILL.md) for capture, Flow,
HyperFrames, media QA, authorized publishing and private archive cleanup.
This skill chooses the story and distribution; it does not duplicate those workflows.

## Decide what deserves publication

Start with the owner's problem, the supported action and the visible result.
Check the deployed feature, its qualification evidence, prerequisites, plan
access and current UI before calling it available. A merged PR, tool name or
provider configuration alone does not establish customer availability. Record
the environment and date of verification. Describe beta, limited rollout and
Sandbox behavior accurately. Do not take real payments, refund customers or
send transaction notifications to produce a demonstration.

Read existing articles, docs and short/provider posts before drafting. Update a
useful existing guide instead of creating another URL for the same instructions.
A genuinely new customer outcome can deserve a launch article; a typo,
maintenance fix or routine doc edit usually needs only the existing page updated.

| Reader's need | Canonical surface | Distribution and timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand a new capability and why to try it | `blog` article | After the affected journey is qualified; consider Product News and a social launch |
| Complete a task or recover from a problem | `docs` article | Ready before the launch links to it; update as the UI changes |
| See one benefit quickly | Short website/social post | Once the linked feature and destination work; tailor the caption to each channel |
| Learn a visible workflow | Actual screenshots, short recording or HyperFrames edit | Include in the article/guide where it resolves a question; excerpt for social |
| Know the state of their booking, payment or account | Existing transactional notification | Generated by the real domain event, never manufactured as a marketing announcement |
| Hear product news they can act on | Existing Product News broadcast | Account for the blog publication trigger below; avoid a second manual send |

For several closely related features, lead with one coherent customer outcome
and link to focused guides. Do not turn every implementation change into its
own launch email. Release when the product and supporting content are ready,
not on an invented daily quota. Follow up with a different use case or answered
question when there is new value; do not repost the same announcement to fill
a calendar. Explicit schedules use the intended audience's timezone and the
actual available scheduling mechanism; this skill creates no recurring task.

## Prepare a human writing handoff

The default deliverable is one focused GitHub writing issue, not an AI-written
article or a bundle of finished channel captions. The owner confirmed the human
writer is GitHub `bamboochow` on 2026-10-07. Verify that account is assignable in
this repository and check for an existing issue before creating another. A new
writer assignment or issue must be authorized by the owner; this skill alone
does not authorize contacting a person. Do not assign an AI agent to write the
article or impersonate the writer's personal experience.

Give the writer a brief they can use without reading the conversation:

1. One audience, one problem and one article angle. State the customer outcome
   in a sentence; omit a kitchen-sink feature list.
2. A working title and a short outline of three or four beats. Describe what
   each beat must establish; let the human choose the language and narrative.
   Use the canonical profile for voice, not a prewritten article to imitate.
3. A short fact sheet with actual prerequisites, limits and direct source links.
   Separate verified behavior from proposed examples and missing evidence.
4. A small media packet: genuine screenshots or recordings placed against the
   outline beat they support, with captions, source environment/date and safe
   accessible links. Provide the actual assets when available, not just a list
   telling the writer to take screenshots. Do not include private customer data.
5. A clear asset decision: screenshot, HyperFrames clip, or neither, and why.
   Name the exact missing capture when an essential screen is inaccessible.
6. Deliverables and finish criteria: a human-written article and summary, one
   CTA to an existing verified destination, factual review and owner acceptance.
   Derive channel excerpts after the article is accepted; do not scatter a brief
   into separate article/social/email writing assignments by default.

Use one canonical issue as the handoff. Keep private working assets in the
approved marketing archive and give the writer verified access through the
appropriate private destination. Public issues may contain already-public
sources/media; do not expose private archive links or grant public sharing to
make an attachment convenient. Include necessary publication consequences in a
short operator note, separate from the outline the human is writing.

When creating the issue, include the confirmed assignee in the create request,
then read it back to verify both the body and assignee. Report the issue URL.
Do not label a handoff complete if the person cannot access essential assets.
If an access or capture is blocked, state the specific gap and finish the useful
outline and verified assets; do not fabricate proof to fill the packet.

The writer owns the prose. The agent owns source checks, media preparation,
factual review and, when authorized, CMS assembly and distribution. Write full
copy only when the owner explicitly asks the agent to do so. A subsequent
publication request does not convert rejected AI copy into accepted human copy.

## When to make a HyperFrames video

Make one when motion clarifies the action or result: selecting a booking time,
seeing availability change, progressing through Checkout, or moving from an
owner's request to a real updated page. A screenshot is better when the reader
needs to inspect a single control; plain prose is enough for a policy change.
Do not generate an avatar or decorative clip just because a feature launched.

Capture the supported journey first. Use HyperFrames to assemble readable
shots, highlight controls, time captions and show the result. Preserve genuine
UI pixels; label illustrative scenes as Demo and never present them as proof
of a live transaction. Use the marketing skill for editing, voice, reusable
brand ending, phone-size QA and provenance. Decide the script, shot list and
channel format before generation. Respect existing owner authorization without
inventing a new per-run approval ritual; this skill grants no new spend or send.

## Publication can send email

Reinspect [article-broadcast.ts](../../../server/domain/article-broadcast.ts),
[scheduled-tasks.ts](../../../server/scheduled-tasks.ts),
[product-news-contacts.ts](../../../server/domain/product-news-contacts.ts) and
[notification-categories.ts](../../../shared/notification-categories.ts) before
a launch. The following describes the implementation inspected on 2026-10-07,
not a claim that a deployed broadcast was sent or received:

- The scheduled article broadcaster runs every five minutes. It selects newly
  first-published, **listed platform `blog` articles** within a 24-hour window
  and claims one broadcast per article. `docs`, tenant articles and short posts
  do not enter this selection. Editing or republishing an already published
  article does not create a fresh announcement.
- In a real-email environment, the canonical sender uses Resend's Product News
  Segment and Topic. The title becomes the subject, summary becomes preview
  text, cover becomes the hero, and text blocks become the email body. Video,
  FAQ and CTA blocks are not carried as article-body text; the page link lets
  the reader reach them. Make the text useful without an embedded video.
- Eligible registered, verified, unbanned users and their Product News email
  preferences determine the audience; reserved test addresses are excluded.
  Product News email currently defaults on. An unsubscribe on either side wins;
  background reconciliation must not re-subscribe the person. Do not describe
  this audience as only people who explicitly opted in unless verified.
- A log-only run is not delivered mail. A stored claim/provider ID is not proof
  of send or receipt. Check the actual provider state and available delivery
  evidence before reporting success. Respect the existing retry window; do not
  alter dates, duplicate articles, edit recipient preferences or create a second
  broadcaster to force delivery.

Before a new listed platform blog publish, make the automatic Product News
consequence explicit in the brief and resolve authorization from the session.
Authorization only for social posting does not authorize this email audience.
If send authorization is missing, finish the reviewed content and assets,
then request the specific missing approval. Do not publish and hope to cancel
the cron afterward. If the owner wants web-only publication, check the current
supported publication controls; do not invent an email-suppression flag or
silently change visibility/collection to bypass the announcement.

Product News is distinct from booking, payment, guest-message, billing and
account-security notifications. Do not use a transactional category to market
a feature or change user preferences. An article publish does not establish a
WhatsApp broadcast or dashboard alert. There is no public broadcast-send tool
in the MCP surface inspected here; use the existing mechanism, not a new tool.

## Publish and distribute through MCP

1. Read `get_workspace_context` and explicitly target the selected organization.
   KrabiClaw's own content uses `organization_id: "platform"`; the active
   workspace may be a customer site. Inspect `list_blog_posts` for the intended
   collection, `list_posts`, and the relevant live provider posts. Reuse the
   exact existing document when appropriate; do not duplicate a launch.
   Listed tools can still be unavailable: inspect every result for `isError`
   and missing structured content. If harmless reads return `Unknown tool`,
   report the connector failure, make at most one confirmation read and finish
   independent content preparation. A local draft is not a CMS draft. Do not
   substitute private API calls or publish through another account to claim an
   MCP acceptance pass. Resume from fresh reads when the connection is restored.
2. Prepare ordered blocks with `create_blog_post` or edit the existing article
   using `get_blog_post` and its latest `expected_updated_at`. Use the correct
   `blog` or `docs` collection and a category from `list_article_categories`.
   Save and place real media using the canonical media tools. Read the draft
   back and inspect its preview before the authorized publish.
3. Publish with `publish_blog_post` and the fresh concurrency token, accounting
   for the Product News consequence above. Open the returned public URL and
   check the final content, images, mobile layout and links. Apply the help-docs
   projection checks for docs. Publication to the website is not social delivery.
4. Read `get_social_connections`; use its exact target IDs, revisions, supported
   formats and operations. A launch article becomes a **separate short post**
   through `create_post`, not another blog entry. Give it a native caption,
   ordered media and one appropriate CTA. Use a new idempotency key per intended
   post and reuse that key only when retrying that same creation. Prepare
   separate posts if Facebook and Instagram need different captions or media.
5. Website announcements can link to the canonical article. Facebook can carry
   the useful destination link. For Instagram, do not promise a clickable caption
   URL; use the verified profile-link destination or a clear domain/next step.
   A short actual demo suits a Reel; a readable image or supported carousel can
   suit a concise explanation. Select formats from the actual connection and
   tool contract, not assumed provider support. Do not add unrequested channels.
6. Call `publish_post` with **exactly** the authorized targets: `organization`
   for the website and selected provider IDs/revisions for social. Continue the
   same post/container when processing; reconcile an unknown outcome with
   `reconcile_post_publication` and live reads before any retry. Do not create a
   new post just because a call timed out. Failed targets remain failed even
   when another channel succeeds.
7. Read `get_post`, then `list_channel_posts`/`get_channel_post` on the selected
   account and inspect the returned public links. Confirm caption, media and
   destination. Record article/post IDs, per-channel outcomes and permalinks in
   the approved private campaign record. Verify the email separately; neither
   a social receipt nor public website status proves an email broadcast.

For a launch, prepare the writer brief, useful sources and media together;
assemble channel excerpts from the accepted human article afterward.
Publish the destination first, verify it, then release its social posts; account
for automatic email timing before the first article publish. Regional variants
need native requests, relevant settings and checked language, not identical
footage with translated captions. Archive sources and QA using the marketing
skill and verify retrieval before authorized local cleanup.

## Example: bookings and payments

Check existing material first: the live platform blog already contains
“Make room for your next booking.” Build on it rather than announce Calendar
as brand new again. Read [Google Calendar](../../../docs/integrations/google-calendar.md)
and [Payments](../../../docs/payments/payments.md) plus current qualification
records. Calendar availability, paid booking, merchant onboarding, Checkout,
refunds and payouts are distinct claims with distinct prerequisites. Sandbox
qualification is not a real customer payment or a promise of every wallet in
every country. Confirm current customer availability and plan before release.

A focused writer outline could follow a service owner making a time bookable and
a guest reserving and paying, **if that complete journey is verified**. Link
the focused booking/payment setup guides. Capture a safe demonstrated flow,
edit one short action-to-result video when it clarifies the story. Assign the
outline and actual assets to the human writer before adapting accepted copy for
Facebook/Instagram.
Use a relevant “Set up bookings” or “Read the guide” destination. Plan one
Product News launch consequence, not separate duplicate emails for each guide.
This example is an editorial plan; it authorizes no content publication,
transaction, generated footage or notification send.
