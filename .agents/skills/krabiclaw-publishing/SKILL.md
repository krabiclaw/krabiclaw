# Publishing KrabiClaw news

Read [product-profile.md](../../../docs/marketing/product-profile.md) for verified
facts and Paul's voice and [PRODUCT.md](../../../PRODUCT.md) for current policy.
Use [write-help-docs](../write-help-docs/SKILL.md) for customer instructions and
[krabiclaw-marketing](../krabiclaw-marketing/SKILL.md) for capture, Flow,
HyperFrames, media QA, authorized publishing and private archive cleanup.
This skill chooses the story, prepares the SEO and editorial foundation, and plans distribution; it does not duplicate those workflows.

## Decide what deserves publication

Start with the owner's problem, the supported action and the visible result.
Check the deployed feature, its qualification evidence, prerequisites, plan
access and current UI before calling it available. A merged PR, tool name or
provider configuration alone does not establish customer availability. Record
the environment and date of verification. Describe beta, limited rollout and
Sandbox behavior accurately. Do not take real payments, refund customers or
send transaction notifications to produce a demonstration.

Read existing articles, docs and short/provider posts before drafting. Update a
useful existing guide instead of creating another URL for the same instructions.
A genuinely new customer outcome can deserve a launch article; a typo,
maintenance fix or routine doc edit usually needs only the existing page updated.

| Reader's need | Canonical surface | Distribution and timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand a new capability and why to try it | `blog` article | After the affected journey is qualified; plan social launch and consider an explicit broadcast |
| Complete a task or recover from a problem | `docs` article | Ready before the launch links to it; update as the UI changes |
| See one benefit quickly | Short website/social post | Once the linked feature and destination work; tailor the caption to each channel |
| Learn a visible workflow | Actual screenshots, short recording or HyperFrames edit | Include in the article/guide where it resolves a question; excerpt for social |
| Know the state of their booking, payment or account | Existing transactional notification | Generated by the real domain event, never manufactured as a marketing announcement |
| Hear product news they can act on | Explicit Product News broadcast | Triggered intentionally via authorized tool or operator action; never a silent cron side-effect |

For several closely related features, lead with one coherent customer outcome
and link to focused guides. Do not turn every implementation change into its
own launch email. Release when the product and supporting content are ready,
not on an invented daily quota. Follow up with a different use case or answered
question when there is new value; do not repost the same announcement to fill
a calendar. Explicit schedules use the intended audience's timezone and the
actual available scheduling mechanism; this skill creates no recurring task.

## Prepare a human writing handoff

The default deliverable is one focused GitHub writing issue, not an AI-written
article or a bundle of finished channel captions. The owner confirmed the human
writer is GitHub `bamboochow` on 2026-10-07. Verify that account is assignable in
this repository and check for an existing issue before creating another. A new
writer assignment or issue must be authorized by the owner; this skill alone
does not authorize contacting a person. Do not assign an AI agent to write the
article or impersonate the writer's personal experience.

Give the writer a brief they can use without reading the conversation:

1. **One audience, one problem and one article angle.** State the customer outcome
   in a single sentence; omit a kitchen-sink feature list.
2. **Working title and beat outline.** 3–4 beats describing what each beat must
   establish; let the human choose the narrative, voice, and phrasing.
3. **Fact sheet and boundary conditions.** Explicit plan requirements (e.g. Basic
   free vs Growth $49/mo vs Business $149/mo, or legacy Commerce $89/mo), country
   availability, payout currency limits, and direct source links. Separate verified
   production behavior from Sandbox or upcoming capabilities.
4. **Comprehensive SEO Research Plan:**
   - **Target Search Intent & Keyword Clusters:** Primary target keyword, secondary
     queries, long-tail problem keywords, and explicit intent classification
     (informational vs commercial vs transactional).
   - **Rich Snippets & Structured Schema:** Target JSON-LD types (`Product`, `Offer`,
     `Event`, `LegalService`, `FAQPage`), specifying the exact attributes the
     article will establish (price currency, availability, geo coordinates, author).
   - **SEO Gap & Competitor Analysis:** Queries where KrabiClaw currently lacks
     visibility, zero-click questions to answer upfront (answer-first formatting in
     the first 40–60 words for Google AI Overviews and Perplexity citations), and
     E-E-A-T grounding.
   - **Internal & Backlink Architecture:** Anchor text and URLs to canonical platform
     surfaces (e.g. `/pricing`, `/services`, `/experiences`), customer showcase sites,
     and targeted industry reference destinations.
5. **Media packet.** Genuine screenshots or recordings placed against the outline
   beat they support, with captions, source environment/date, and accessible links.
   State the exact missing capture when an essential screen is inaccessible.
6. **Deliverables and finish criteria.** A human-written article and summary, one
   CTA to an existing verified destination, factual review, and owner acceptance.
   Derive channel excerpts after the article is accepted; do not scatter a brief
   into separate article/social/email writing assignments by default.

## When to make a HyperFrames video

Make one when motion clarifies the action or result: selecting a booking time,
seeing availability change, progressing through Checkout, or moving from an
owner's request to a real updated page. A screenshot is better when the reader
needs to inspect a single control; plain prose is enough for a policy change.
Do not generate an avatar or decorative clip just because a feature launched.

Capture the supported journey first. Use HyperFrames to assemble readable
shots, highlight controls, time captions and show the result. Preserve genuine
UI pixels; label illustrative scenes as Demo and never present them as proof
of a live transaction. Use the marketing skill for editing, voice, reusable
brand ending, phone-size QA and provenance. Decide the script, shot list and
channel format before generation. Respect existing owner authorization without
inventing a new per-run approval ritual; this skill grants no new spend or send.

## Email broadcasts are explicit, not automatic

Publishing a platform `blog` or `docs` article is decoupled from email broadcasting.
There is no background cron job broadcasting newly published articles to subscriber lists.
An article publish creates or updates the website and RSS/feed representation only.

When an email broadcast to the Product News list is desired:
- It requires **explicit owner authorization** and is dispatched via an intentional
  operator command or authorized tool call, never as a silent background side-effect.
- In a real-email environment, the canonical sender uses Resend's Product News
  Segment and Topic. The title becomes the subject, summary becomes preview
  text, cover becomes the hero, and text blocks become the email body.
- Video, FAQ, and CTA blocks are not carried directly in email markup; the article
  link allows the reader to reach the full interactive surface.
- An explicit broadcast must check recipient preferences and excluded test addresses.
  A stored claim or provider ID is not proof of receipt; verify actual provider status.

## Publish and distribute through MCP

1. **Verify organization context:** Read `get_workspace_context` and explicitly
   target the selected organization. KrabiClaw's own content uses `organization_id: "platform"`;
   the active workspace may be a customer site. Inspect `list_blog_posts` for the intended
   collection, `list_posts`, and the relevant live provider posts. Reuse the
   exact existing document when appropriate; do not duplicate a launch.
   Listed tools can still be unavailable: inspect every result for `isError`
   and missing structured content. If harmless reads return `Unknown tool`,
   report the connector failure, make at most one confirmation read and finish
   independent content preparation. Resume from fresh reads when the connection is restored.
2. **Stage CMS draft:** Prepare ordered blocks with `create_blog_post` (keeping it as
   an unpublished draft until copy is approved) or edit the existing article using
   `get_blog_post` and its latest `expected_updated_at`. Use the correct `blog` or
   `docs` collection and a category from `list_article_categories`. Save and place real
   media using canonical media tools.
3. **Publish when authorized:** Publish with `publish_blog_post` and the fresh concurrency
   token. Open the returned public URL and check the final content, images, mobile layout,
   links, and structured schema. Publication to the website is not social delivery.
4. **Channel social posts:** Read `get_social_connections`; use its exact target IDs,
   revisions, supported formats and operations. A launch article becomes a **separate short post**
   through `create_post`, not another blog entry. Give it a native caption, ordered media
   and one appropriate CTA. Use a unique idempotency key per intended post.
5. **Tailor destinations:** Website announcements can link to the canonical article.
   Facebook can carry the useful destination link. For Instagram, do not promise a
   clickable caption URL; use the verified profile-link destination or a clear domain/next step.
   A short actual demo suits a Reel; a readable image or supported carousel can suit a concise explanation.
6. **Publish social targets:** Call `publish_post` with **exactly** the authorized targets:
   `organization` for the website and selected provider IDs/revisions for social. Continue the
   same post/container when processing; reconcile an unknown outcome with
   `reconcile_post_publication` and live reads before any retry. Do not create a new post
   just because a call timed out.
7. **Read back verification:** Read `get_post`, then `list_channel_posts`/`get_channel_post`
   on the selected account and inspect the returned public links. Confirm caption, media,
   and destination. Record article/post IDs, per-channel outcomes, and permalinks in the
   campaign log.

## Example: bookings and payments

Check existing material first: the live platform blog already contains
“Make room for your next booking.” Build on it rather than announce Calendar
as brand new again. Read [Google Calendar](../../../docs/integrations/google-calendar.md)
and [Payments](../../../docs/payments/payments.md) plus current qualification
records.

Plan entitlement: New customers need **Business ($149/month)** for online payments
and direct paid stay bookings, plus a ready Stripe connected account. Basic (free)
and Growth ($49/month) retain supported bookings without online payments; a manually
recorded stay is not a guest payment. Legacy Commerce ($89/month) remains an active
grandfathered contract.

Growth and Business include 2 business locations, then $49 per additional location
per month, and separately 2 live homes, then $9 per additional home per month,
billed at period end. A home's location does not count again as a business
location. Basic includes 2 of each and refuses a third. Live means active at an
active location and published on the site or syncing through an enabled Channex
mapping. Draft homes are free and unlimited; existing subscriptions without the
homes price keep their contracted prices. Read the target environment's catalog
before publishing pricing.

Multi-vertical reality and geographic boundaries:
- **Experiences (Pottery House, Kikuzuki):** Experience operators can collect pre-payments
  for workshops (e.g. Pottery House pottery wheel classes) or ticketed dining/omakase sessions.
  For Thai merchants, Stripe Connect accounts operate in THB with local Thai bank account
  payouts on a T+7 schedule; cross-border Express onboarding must respect Bank of Thailand
  regulatory requirements.
- **Legal Services (Blawby / NCLS):** Solo attorneys and law firms can require pre-payment
  for initial legal case evaluations and consultations in USD.
- **Lodging:** Hosts manage homes, nightly prices, availability and stay rules,
  direct paid stay bookings, private arrival guides and scheduled guest messages.
  A configured Airbnb connection through Channex applies to the selected account
  and mapped homes. Demonstrate the actual connection and delivery outcome; do
  not promise every booking channel or label direct stay payments as upcoming.
  The iOS app is a free account companion where distribution access is available;
  verify access before linking a download or announcing an App Store release.

A focused writer outline follows an operator configuring their bookable session,
a guest reserving and paying, and the confirmed calendar hold converting to a confirmed booking,
**with verified sandbox evidence**. Assign the outline, verified SEO plan, and genuine
assets to human writer `bamboochow` before adapting accepted copy for social channels.
