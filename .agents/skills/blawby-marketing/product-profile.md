## Positioning

Sitewide positioning: **“Run your business from your favorite AI app with Blawby.”**
Blawby is the business AI agent. The customer works in ChatGPT, Claude, or another compatible AI app; Blawby connects their request to business records and supported actions. Connected services such as Airbnb, Google Calendar, social accounts, and analytics are integrations. “Your business’s second brain” describes the business context Blawby uses. MCP belongs in setup instructions and technical documentation.

## What Blawby does

Blawby runs the website, bookings, payments and messaging for restaurants,
experience operators, law firms and lodging hosts. Owners manage it from the
dashboard or from Claude, ChatGPT, and compatible AI apps, with the same
permissions either way.

- Every business gets a website on its own brand, with pages, menus or
  offerings, locations, media and translations.
- Law firms take consultation requests, booked against each attorney's working
  hours and Google Calendar busy times.
- Lodging hosts manage listings, nightly availability and prices, stays and guest
  conversations. Airbnb connects through Channex (requires Business plan). Vrbo connects by calendar
  link only: occupied dates, not prices, guest details or messages.
- The iOS app is a free companion for hosts and teams.

Claims to avoid unless checked against the product first: identical file
attachments in every MCP client (ChatGPT's file handling is what is supported),
automatic social sync, ranking or sales results, measured time savings, and
public ChatGPT directory approval.

## Voice

Paul's writing ([multi-touch attribution](https://becominghuman.ai/multi-touch-attribution-in-digital-marketing-and-why-you-should-stop-caring-about-it-d246ef773990),
[Kirirom](https://medium.com/@PaulChrisLuke/building-the-tech-future-of-cambodia-in-a-national-park-kirirom-d05555e0f271))
starts with a concrete observation, explains the mechanism, finds the
commercial opportunity, then connects it to people. Technology and money serve
people.

- Show the task and its result; don't teach basics or tell viewers what is
  useful. "Need to get your menu online? Take a picture of your menu, give it to
  Blawby, done!" is the cadence.
- Use everyday examples to make a mechanism tangible. Self-deprecating asides
  only when they come naturally.
- First-person stories only when they happened. No invented anecdotes, no
  generic AI copy, no architecture lists.
- Feature posts and help docs show the real process: chat footage for
  conversational features, dashboard recordings for visual ones.
- Thai and Japanese videos are their own commercials, with their own
  businesses, casting and rhythm, not the English cut with translated voices.

## Founder story

**Long version** (About page, interviews, launch posts)

```text
Blawby grew out of a problem I first encountered during COVID. Restaurant owners and tour operators I knew suddenly needed online ordering, bookings, and dependable websites. We built an early open-source product to help them adapt quickly.

The software worked, but maintaining the websites did not scale. Many owners had no interest in learning another CMS—and they should not have needed to. They would send us photos of menus or new dishes, and we would visit their businesses to take more photos, organize the content, and update everything ourselves. We helped where we could, but that service-heavy model became impossible to maintain.

AI changed what was possible. With ChatGPT and modern agent tools, restaurant owners could describe a new item or ask for a change in plain language to keep their website current.

I returned to the idea in May 2026 and built what is now Blawby. It helps restaurants, tour operators, law firms and lodging hosts manage content, bookings, inquiries, products, media, and translations through AI while preserving the fundamentals that make a website effective: structured content, multilingual pages, and clear paths for customers.

My name is Paul Chris Luke, and my background is in marketing. Blawby combines that marketing discipline with a simpler way for business owners to maintain the information their customers depend on. The goal is straightforward: give local businesses a website that performs well without turning website administration into another job.
```

**One paragraph** (directories, compact forms)

```text
Blawby grew from an open-source product we built during COVID to help restaurants and tour operators add online ordering and bookings. The first version proved the need, but manually maintaining client menus, photos, and content did not scale. AI made the natural workflow possible: owners send a photo or describe a change, and Blawby keeps a structured, multilingual website current.
```

The COVID-era product was a predecessor, not Blawby; don't use it as Blawby's
start date.
